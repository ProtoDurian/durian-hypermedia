## Tier list: your own tiers

The tiers are S, A, B, C and D unless you say otherwise. **A list may have any tiers, in any number.** Start with a line `tiers:` that names them; a name may be followed by a colour in square brackets (`c1` to `c8`, `good`, `warn` or `bad`). The tier lines use those names; a tier with no line is an empty row. This list has four tiers.

```tierlist title="What the first release needs"
tiers: Must [good], Should [c6], Could [c3], Won't [bad]
Must: Sign in, Pay by card (one provider)
Should: Email receipts, A dark theme
Could: Gift cards (needs a legal check)
Won't: A mobile app
```
