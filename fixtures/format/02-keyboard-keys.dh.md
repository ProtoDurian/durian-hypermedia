## Keyboard keys

One key :kbd[Esc], a combination :kbd[Ctrl+Shift+P], and a sequence :kbd[Ctrl+K, Ctrl+S]: press the first, let go, then press the second.
Arrow keys are written by name: :kbd[Left] and :kbd[Right] step through a stage; the page draws the arrows.
